About Mathura

Mathura is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is famous for its religious heritage and temples.

Famous Places

  • Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi
  • Dwarkadhish Temple
  • Vishram Ghat

Culture and Festivals

Mathura is famous for Janmashtami, Holi, traditional music, and religious celebrations.
